NUTRITION MONTH

Healthy Diet, Healthy Life

What is Nutrition Month?

Nutrition Month is celebrated every July in the Philippines. It reminds everyone to eat healthy foods and practice good eating habits to live a healthier life.

Balanced Diet

A balanced diet includes the right amount of carbohydrates, protein, fruits, vegetables, and healthy fats. Eating a variety of foods every day helps our body get the nutrients it needs to grow, stay active, and remain healthy.

Healthy Foods

Fruits, vegetables, fish, eggs, milk, rice, and other nutritious foods help our body become stronger and protect us from sickness.

Healthy Lifestyle

Eating healthy meals, drinking enough water, exercising, and getting enough sleep are simple ways to stay healthy every day.

Conclusion

Good nutrition is important for every student. A healthy body helps us study better, stay active, and enjoy life with our family and friends.
