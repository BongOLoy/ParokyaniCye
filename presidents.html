PHILIPPINE PRESIDENTS

1899 – 1901

Emilio Aguinaldo

Full name: Emilio Aguinaldo y Famy

AGE AT DEATH: 94 years old

NOTABLE CONTRIBUTION: Established the First Philippine Republic in 1899 and led the government that adopted the Malolos Constitution.

1935 – 1944

Manuel L. Quezon

Full name: Manuel Luis Quezon

AGE AT DEATH: 65 years old

NOTABLE CONTRIBUTION: Helped establish the Commonwealth government and promoted the development of a national language and social justice programs.

1943 – 1945

Jose P. Laurel

Full name: José Paciano Laurel y García

AGE AT DEATH: 68 years old

NOTABLE CONTRIBUTION: Led the Japanese-sponsored Second Philippine Republic during World War II. His presidency took place under occupation and remains a complex, controversial period in Philippine history.

1944 – 1946

Sergio Osmeña

Full name: Sergio Osmeña Sr.

AGE AT DEATH: 83 years old

NOTABLE CONTRIBUTION: Led the Commonwealth government after Quezon's death and helped guide the country through liberation and postwar reconstruction.

1946 – 1948

Manuel Roxas

Full name: Manuel Acuña Roxas

AGE AT DEATH: 56 years old

NOTABLE CONTRIBUTION: Became the first president of the independent Third Republic in 1946 and led the country's early postwar rebuilding.

1948 – 1953

Elpidio Quirino

Full name: Elpidio Rivera Quirino

AGE AT DEATH: 65 years old

NOTABLE CONTRIBUTION: Promoted postwar rehabilitation and rural development, including programs intended to improve housing and conditions for farmers.

1953 – 1957

Ramon Magsaysay

Full name: Ramon del Fierro Magsaysay

AGE AT DEATH: 49 years old

NOTABLE CONTRIBUTION: Strengthened rural development and land-reform efforts, while his administration helped weaken the Huk rebellion and made the government more accessible to ordinary citizens.

1957 – 1961

Carlos P. Garcia

Full name: Carlos Polestico Garcia

AGE AT DEATH: 74 years old

NOTABLE CONTRIBUTION: Introduced the Filipino First Policy, which encouraged giving Filipino businesses and workers priority in the national economy.

1961 – 1965

Diosdado Macapagal

Full name: Diosdado Pangan Macapagal

AGE AT DEATH: 86 years old

NOTABLE CONTRIBUTION: Signed the Agricultural Land Reform Code of 1963 and moved the celebration of Philippine Independence Day to June 12.

1965 – 1986

Ferdinand Marcos

Full name: Ferdinand Emmanuel Edralin Marcos

AGE AT DEATH: 72 years old

NOTABLE CONTRIBUTION: Oversaw major infrastructure projects, including roads, bridges, and public buildings. His administration also imposed Martial Law and was marked by authoritarian rule and human-rights abuses.

1986 – 1992

Corazon C. Aquino

Full name: Maria Corazon Sumulong Cojuangco Aquino

AGE AT DEATH: 76 years old

NOTABLE CONTRIBUTION: Restored democratic institutions after the 1986 People Power Revolution and oversaw the ratification of the 1987 Constitution.

1992 – 1998

Fidel V. Ramos

Full name: Fidel Valdez Ramos

AGE AT DEATH: 94 years old

NOTABLE CONTRIBUTION: Helped end the power crisis and signed the 1996 peace agreement with the Moro National Liberation Front, while pursuing economic reforms.

1998 – 2001

Joseph Estrada

Full name: Jose Marcelo Ejercito

STILL LIVING

NOTABLE CONTRIBUTION: Promoted the Lingap para sa Mahirap program, which aimed to provide assistance and basic services to poor communities.

2001 – 2010

Gloria Macapagal Arroyo

Full name: Gloria Macaraeg Macapagal Arroyo

STILL LIVING

NOTABLE CONTRIBUTION: Launched the 4Ps conditional cash transfer program in 2007 to support the health and education of low-income families.

2010 – 2016

Benigno Aquino III

Full name: Benigno Simeon Cojuangco Aquino III

AGE AT DEATH: 61 years old

NOTABLE CONTRIBUTION: Oversaw the passage of the Sin Tax Reform Act and the K–12 education reform, and pursued a peace agreement with the Moro Islamic Liberation Front.

2016 – 2022

Rodrigo Duterte

Full name: Rodrigo Roa Duterte

STILL LIVING

NOTABLE CONTRIBUTION: Signed the Universal Health Care Act and the Bangsamoro Organic Law, and launched the Build, Build, Build infrastructure program.

2022 – Present

Ferdinand Marcos Jr.

Full name: Ferdinand Romualdez Marcos Jr.

STILL LIVING

NOTABLE CONTRIBUTION: His administration has continued major infrastructure projects through the Build Better More program. His term is ongoing, so his overall record is still developing.
