Diosdado Pangan Macapagal

PRESIDENT • 1961 – 1965

BIOGRAPHY

Diosdado Pangan Macapagal was born on September 28, 1910, in Lubao, Pampanga, Philippines. He came from a poor family and worked hard to continue his education. Despite financial difficulties, he completed his studies in law at the University of Santo Tomas. He later earned advanced degrees in law and economics and became one of the top performers in the 1936 Bar Examination.

Macapagal began his professional career as a lawyer and later entered government service. In 1949, he was elected as a representative of Pampanga. He served in the House of Representatives for several years before becoming vice president in 1957 under President Carlos P. Garcia. He also became the president of the Liberal Party.

His experience in law and government helped prepare him for national leadership. In the 1961 presidential election, Macapagal defeated incumbent President Carlos P. Garcia and became the ninth president of the Philippines.

Presidency and Land Reform

During his presidency, Macapagal focused on fighting poverty, improving the economy, and addressing problems faced by Filipino farmers. One of his most important achievements was signing the Agricultural Land Reform Code in 1963. This law aimed to improve the lives of farmers by changing the old system of land tenancy and supporting a fairer arrangement between landowners and farmers.

Macapagal also made an important change to the celebration of Philippine Independence Day. In 1964, he officially moved the date from July 4 to June 12, honoring the declaration of independence made by Emilio Aguinaldo in 1898. His administration also worked to strengthen the country's economy and improve government honesty.

Later Life and Legacy

Macapagal served as president until 1965, when he lost the election to Ferdinand Marcos Sr. After leaving office, he continued to take part in public affairs. In 1971, he became president of the Constitutional Convention, which was responsible for preparing a new constitution.

Diosdado Macapagal died on April 21, 1997, in Makati City. He is remembered for his efforts to improve the lives of farmers, strengthen the economy, and recognize June 12 as the country's Independence Day.
