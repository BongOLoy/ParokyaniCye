Ferdinand Romualdez Marcos Jr.

PRESIDENT • 2022 – PRESENT

BIOGRAPHY

Early Life and Family Background

Ferdinand Romualdez Marcos Jr., commonly known as Bongbong Marcos or BBM, was born on September 13, 1957, in Manila, Philippines. He is the son of former President Ferdinand Marcos Sr. and former First Lady Imelda Romualdez Marcos. He grew up during his father's political career and spent part of his childhood in Malacañang Palace.

Marcos studied in several schools, including institutions in the Philippines and abroad. He attended Oxford University in the United Kingdom, where he completed a special diploma in social studies. He also studied at the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania but did not complete its graduate business program.

Beginning of His Political Career

Marcos entered politics in 1980 when he became vice governor of Ilocos Norte. In 1983, he became governor of the province, a position he held until 1986. His early political career took place during the final years of his father's administration.

Following the 1986 People Power Revolution, his family left the Philippines and went to the United States. They returned to the country in 1991. Marcos later continued his political career, becoming a representative of Ilocos Norte's Second District in the House of Representatives in 1992.

He served as governor of Ilocos Norte again from 1998 to 2007. In 2010, he was elected senator and served until 2016. During his time in the Senate, he participated in legislative work involving local government, public services, and national development.

Election and Presidency

In 2022, Marcos ran for president with Sara Duterte as his vice-presidential running mate. His campaign emphasized national unity, economic recovery, infrastructure development, and continuity of some programs from previous administrations.

He won the 2022 presidential election and became the seventeenth President of the Philippines on June 30, 2022. His administration introduced the slogan "Bagong Pilipinas," which presented its goal of building a more progressive, united, and developed country.

Economic Programs and Development

One of the major priorities of Marcos's administration has been economic recovery and investment. His government has worked to attract businesses, encourage tourism, support local industries, and create employment opportunities.

His administration also continued infrastructure development through the "Build Better More" program. It includes projects involving roads, bridges, airports, railways, and other public facilities intended to improve transportation and connect communities.

The Philippines recorded economic growth during the early years of his administration. However, many Filipino households continued to experience difficulties with the cost of food, electricity, transportation, and other basic needs. This led to criticism that economic growth did not always translate into easier daily living for ordinary citizens.

Education, Food, and Social Welfare

Marcos's administration has supported the construction and improvement of public school classrooms and facilities. It has also continued programs intended to improve access to education and provide better learning environments for students.

Another program introduced by his administration is Walang Gutom 2027, a food assistance initiative intended to help food-poor families obtain nutritious food. The government has also continued social welfare assistance and school-based feeding programs.

These programs represent efforts to address poverty, hunger, and education concerns. However, their long-term success depends on proper implementation, sufficient funding, and making sure that assistance reaches the families who need it most.

Foreign Relations and International Cooperation

Marcos has worked to strengthen the Philippines' relationships with other countries, particularly the United States, Japan, and Southeast Asian nations. His administration has emphasized international cooperation, trade, investment, and national security.

His government has also maintained the Philippines' position on the West Philippine Sea. The administration has continued to raise concerns about maritime activities in the country's exclusive economic zone and has sought support from international partners.

Flood Control Corruption Controversy

One of the biggest controversies during Marcos's presidency involved alleged corruption and irregularities in government flood control projects. In 2025, investigations and public hearings revealed serious concerns about projects that were reportedly incomplete, substandard, or possibly nonexistent despite receiving government funding.

Marcos himself announced that thousands of flood control projects needed closer examination. He also identified major contractors that had received large amounts of government project funding. Some contractors became controversial after reports about their expensive collections of luxury vehicles, including high-end cars such as Rolls-Royce and Lamborghini models.

The controversy angered many Filipinos because flood control funds are supposed to protect communities, homes, and livelihoods from flooding. Critics questioned how public money intended for disaster protection could be lost through alleged corruption while ordinary families continued to suffer from floods.

Marcos ordered investigations, audits, and efforts to recover public funds. His administration also established the Independent Commission for Infrastructure to investigate irregularities in government infrastructure projects. However, these actions did not remove criticism of his leadership. Many people argued that the government should have prevented these problems earlier and demanded that all responsible officials and contractors be held accountable.

It is important to distinguish the allegations against contractors and government officials from proven responsibility. The existence of corruption investigations does not automatically prove that Marcos personally stole money or purchased luxury vehicles using public funds. Nevertheless, because these controversies involved projects implemented during his presidency, questions about government supervision, accountability, and leadership became major issues.

Other Criticisms and Challenges

Marcos has also faced criticism over the country's continuing poverty, high living costs, and problems involving agriculture. Rice prices and the cost of basic goods have remained important concerns for many Filipino families. Critics have questioned whether government policies have done enough to make food more affordable and support local farmers.

Another concern has been the country's education system. Despite efforts to build classrooms and improve school facilities, overcrowded classrooms, shortages of learning materials, and difficulties in educational quality have continued to affect many schools.

His administration has also faced political disagreements and public criticism over government spending, policy decisions, and the handling of national issues. These challenges have led some Filipinos to question whether the government is focusing enough on the everyday needs of ordinary citizens.

These criticisms do not mean that every government decision was unsuccessful. However, they show that a president must be judged not only by announcements and promises, but also by the actual results of policies and the way public money is managed.

Positive Contributions and Achievements

Despite the controversies, Marcos's administration has also pursued programs that may benefit the country. Infrastructure development, investment promotion, classroom construction, food assistance, and efforts to improve international relationships are among its major initiatives.

His government has also continued efforts to modernize public services and encourage digital transactions. These initiatives aim to make government services more accessible and improve the way people and businesses interact with public institutions.

The administration's infrastructure and social welfare programs are important efforts, although their true success should be measured by their results. Projects must be completed properly, assistance must reach deserving families, and public funds must be protected from misuse.

Continuing Presidency and Legacy

As the seventeenth President of the Philippines, Ferdinand Marcos Jr. began his six-year term on June 30, 2022. His presidency is ongoing, and its final historical assessment will depend on the results of his administration, the challenges it faces, and the decisions made before his term ends in 2028.

Marcos's presidency has included efforts to develop infrastructure, strengthen international relationships, support education, and provide assistance to poor families. At the same time, it has faced serious criticism involving the cost of living, public services, and alleged corruption in government projects.

His leadership remains a subject of public debate. Supporters recognize his administration's development programs, while critics demand stronger accountability, better results, and greater protection of taxpayers' money. His legacy will depend not simply on what his government promises, but on what it actually delivers to the Filipino people.

Leadership is not measured by promises alone, but by responsibility, honest service, and the results experienced by the people.
