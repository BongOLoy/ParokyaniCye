Where Did the Days of the Week Come From?

Have you ever wondered where the names Monday, Tuesday, or Sunday came from? The names of the days have been used for a very long time and came from ancient cultures and their gods.

Where Did They Come From?

Sunday was named after the Sun, and Monday was named after the Moon. Tuesday to Friday were influenced by Germanic and Norse gods. Saturday came from the Roman god Saturn.

Fun Fact

So, the days of the week were not invented by one person. Their names developed over hundreds of years from different cultures.
