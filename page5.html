THE REFRIGERATOR

Keeping Food Fresh and Cool

Why is a Refrigerator Important?

A refrigerator is an important appliance in our homes. It keeps food and drinks cold and helps slow down food spoilage. Because of this, food can stay fresh for a longer period of time.

Who Invented the Refrigerator?

The refrigerator was developed through the work of several inventors. William Cullen demonstrated an early artificial refrigeration device in 1748. Later, Jacob Perkins patented an early practical vapor-compression refrigeration machine in 1834.

Why We Need It

Refrigerators help us store food safely and reduce food waste. They are now commonly found in homes, restaurants, stores, and other places.
