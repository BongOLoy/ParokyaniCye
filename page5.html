THE REFRIGERATOR

Keeping Food Fresh and Cool

Why Is a Refrigerator Important?

A refrigerator is an important appliance in our homes. It keeps food and drinks cold and helps slow down food spoilage. Because of this, food can stay fresh for a longer period of time.

It also helps preserve items such as milk, fruits, vegetables, meat, and leftovers. Keeping these foods at a safe temperature helps slow the growth of many harmful bacteria.

Who Invented the Refrigerator?

The refrigerator was developed through the work of several inventors. William Cullen demonstrated an early artificial refrigeration device in 1748. Later, Jacob Perkins patented an early practical vapor-compression refrigeration machine in 1834.

Over time, refrigeration technology improved, making refrigerators more practical for everyday use. They eventually became common household appliances.

How Does a Refrigerator Work?

A refrigerator removes heat from its inside and releases it into the surrounding air. It uses a special substance called a refrigerant that circulates through the cooling system. This process helps keep the inside of the refrigerator cold.

Why Do We Need It?

Refrigerators help us store food safely and reduce food waste. They are now commonly found in homes, restaurants, stores, and other places.

Fun Fact: Refrigerators do not make food last forever. Food can still spoil, so it is important to keep the correct temperature and use stored food within a safe period.
